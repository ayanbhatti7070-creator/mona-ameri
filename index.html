Proposed About section

At Salt & Silk Luxury, the details make the gathering. I create thoughtfully curated grazing experiences and gifts for the moments people want to make memorable, from corporate celebrations and client appreciation to real estate showcases, team gatherings, and personal occasions. The work goes beyond arranging beautiful food. Each concept is shaped around the occasion, the guests, and the atmosphere you want to create. From bespoke grazing boards and curated gift boxes to styled food presentations for events, the goal is to make hosting feel polished and considered, without adding unnecessary work for your team. Salt & Silk works with businesses, real estate professionals, event planners, venues, and hosts across the Las Vegas area. Depending on the experience, offerings can include tailored menus, custom presentation, delivery, setup, and pickup. Planning a corporate gathering, client gift, open house, or celebration? Send me a message here on LinkedIn and tell me what you are planning. We can shape an experience around the occasion.

Three banner directions
01

Editorial luxury

Message: Thoughtful Gatherings. Beautifully Curated.

Composition: A detailed grazing spread on the right, headline in a quiet dark field on the left, restrained cream and muted gold typography.

Palette/type: Deep olive, warm ivory, antique gold. Elegant serif headline with clean sans-serif support line.

Safe area: Keep all text away from the far left lower corner where the profile photo may overlap. Leave generous margins for mobile cropping.

02

Corporate hosting made considered

Message: Make Your Next Gathering Feel Thoughtfully Different.

Composition: A clean tabletop scene with a refined board and gift box, minimal props, headline across the central-left region.

Palette/type: Stone, charcoal green, soft cream. High-contrast serif title and compact sans-serif service descriptor.

Safe area: Keep headline and key products inside the central 70% of the canvas.

03

Gifting with intention

Message: Client Gifts They Will Remember.

Composition: A premium curated gift box with ribbon, artisan accompaniments, and a small grazing board. Use the right third for the product and left side for the message.

Palette/type: Warm taupe, olive, cream, a subtle brass accent. Refined serif headline and restrained sans-serif subline.

Safe area: Keep text clear of the left profile-image overlap and avoid placing important details at the far edges.
